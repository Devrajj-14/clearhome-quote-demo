# Side-income outreach project

This repository contains a transparent sample landing page for home cleaning businesses. Open `index.html` in a browser. The form builds a quote message preview; it deliberately does not send data or use a real business number.

## Offer

**WhatsApp quote page for local service businesses**

- A responsive one-page site with services, area, contact details, and a short quote form.
- The form opens the client's WhatsApp number with a structured message after the client approves the final copy and provides their number.
- Client supplies verified business facts, branding, photos, service areas, and phone number.
- Suggested starting quote: ₹9,000 for setup, two revision rounds, and 30 days of minor fixes. Domain, hosting, paid integrations, copywriting from scratch, and ongoing maintenance are separate.
- Optional monthly maintenance: ₹1,500, only if agreed. Do not promise lead volume or ranking.

## Delivery checklist

1. Confirm scope and client identity in writing; collect a deposit before custom work.
2. Collect approved copy, photos, phone number, and service details.
3. Replace sample branding and disclosures with approved facts.
4. Set the WhatsApp target number, test form on mobile, confirm all links and accessibility.
5. Have the client review the live preview; publish after approval and payment terms are met.
